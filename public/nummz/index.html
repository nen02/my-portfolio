nummz

A private-first personal finance manager. Track expenses, manage budgets, monitor debts — all stored locally on your device.

Android iOS In Development

nummz is currently in active development. Features and UI are subject to change before release.

📊

Dashboard

Get a complete overview of your finances at a glance — total balance, recent transactions, spending trends, and upcoming payments all in one place.

🎯

Budget Tracking

Set monthly budgets per category and watch your spending in real time. Visual progress bars make it easy to see where you stand before the month ends.

💳

Credit Cards

Track credit card balances and outstanding amounts separately from your cash accounts. Log payments and see exactly how much you owe at any time.

🤝

Debt Management

Keep track of money you've lent to others and amounts you've borrowed. Record repayments and see outstanding balances clearly separated.

🗓️

Scheduled Payments & Shopping

Never miss a recurring bill — schedule payments and receive reminders. Plan your grocery runs and purchases with the built-in shopping list.

⚙️

Settings & Data Control

Customize the app to your preferences. Export an encrypted backup at any time and restore it on a new device — your data never leaves your hands.

🛠️

Built With

nummz is built with React Native and Expo for a smooth cross-platform experience. All data is stored in a local SQLite database — no account required, no internet connection needed.

React Native Expo TypeScript SQLite expo-sqlite expo-notifications
