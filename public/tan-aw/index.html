Moments, pinned to places.

A map-first social app. Share photos and little moments where they happened, and explore your friends' moments on a cute, custom-styled map. The name is Filipino for "to look".

iOS first Android next In Development
🗺️

The map is the product

Not a feed with a map bolted on. Everything starts on a custom "Lagoon Vivid" map style.

  • Moments show up as Bo emoji pins that cluster as you zoom out
  • Moments at the same spot open into a ring of pins
  • Tappable landmarks: parks, malls, museums, stations
  • A day map and a softer night map
📸

Share a moment

A two-step composer: pick photos and an emoji, then pin it.

  • Up to 4 photos from your library or camera
  • Name a real place from the search, or type your own
  • On the map for 24 hours, then it lives on your profile
  • You choose who sees it: everyone, friends or only you
👋

Friends & following

Keep up with people you know, and follow the ones you don't.

  • A circles strip shows who has moments on the map right now
  • Mutual friends, plus one-way follows for public moments
  • Likes, comments and replies, all collected in Activity
  • People you may know, never suggested from your location
🛡️

Private by design

A map app that's careful about where you are.

  • Approximate location, used only to move the camera
  • Photo location data (EXIF/GPS) stripped on upload
  • Exact spots stay visible only to whoever posted them
  • Quiet blocking, reporting and a 30-day account undo

Meet Bo 👀

Bo is the pal: a round, lagoon-teal character who peeks over things (the name comes from peek-a-boo).

Bo is the app icon, the logo and every emoji: 13 faces and 14 activities, all drawn in code and animated with Reanimated. On the map, Bo hides behind bushes in parks and pops out to say hi when tapped.
